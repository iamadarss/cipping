Standard Production Workflow (6 Steps)

Follow this sequential path from raw footage to published viral content.

01

Import or Download Video

Upload local video files (MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM) or paste a YouTube URL in the Downloader to pull high-res DASH streams merged via FFmpeg.

02

Choose Aspect & Framework

Select your target format: 9:16 Shorts/Reels, 1:1 Square, 4:5 Portrait, or 16:9 Landscape. Center-weighted reframing ensures subjects stay in view.

03

AI Highlight & Scene Detection

PySceneDetect scans frame transitions while Whisper AI isolates high-energy speech moments and transcripts to carve out captivating hooks.

04

Style Animated Captions

Choose from curated presets (Clean, Bold, Creator, Gaming, Podcast) or customize font, color, word-by-word karaoke, outline, and pop animations.

05

Review & Multi-Track Edit

Preview generated cuts in the Review Grid or open them in the Editor / Caption Studio for precision trimming, line splitting, and reordering.

06

Export & Distribute

Export 1080p videos with burned ASS subtitles, download individual clips or a full ZIP archive, or schedule direct uploads via YouTube Desk.

Step-by-Step Practical Guides

Click any topic below to expand comprehensive instructions and best practices.

  1. Navigate to AI Clip Studio (/dashboard) or click New Project.
  2. In Step 01, drop your source video or select one from the input library. Click the 9:16 Shorts aspect ratio card.
  3. Click Next: Settings to proceed to Step 02.
  4. Expand Clipping Settings to choose desired minimum and maximum duration (e.g. 15s to 60s) and target clip count.
  5. Expand Subtitles & Captions to select your language and subtitle style preset. Enable Animated Karaoke Captions for active word pop animations.
  6. Click Start Processing. UpClip Studio will transcribe audio with Whisper AI, run PySceneDetect, center-crop, and generate clips.
  7. In Step 04, review your generated clips, trim any excess, or transfer directly to Caption Studio for fine-tuning.
  • Open Caption Studio (/caption-studio) and ensure your media file is loaded.
  • If you don't have captions yet, click the Auto Caption button in the top bar to transcribe your speech with AI.
  • Seek-on-Click: Click any caption card or timing input in the left pane to immediately jump the video playhead to that exact moment.
  • Split & Merge: Click the ✂ button on a card to split that segment at the current playhead position, or use the context menu to merge adjacent cards.
  • Live Repositioning: Drag the caption box directly inside the interactive video canvas to position it perfectly within TikTok / Instagram safe title areas.
  • Inspector Styling: Use the right pane to select modern fonts (Anton, Inter, Poppins, Bebas Neue), configure outline strokes, active highlight colors, and background boxes.
  • Click Export Video to burn your styled captions with FFmpeg, or click Export SRT/VTT to download subtitle files including ASS.
  1. Open the YouTube Downloader (/yt-downloader).
  2. Paste any valid YouTube video, Shorts, or playlist URL into the input field and click Analyze.
  3. Review metadata: title, duration, view count, channel name, and available format resolutions.
  4. Choose your format pill (e.g. 1080p Full HD, 720p HD, or MP3 Audio). UpClip Studio automatically fetches separate video and audio DASH streams and merges them using your local FFmpeg binary.
  5. Click Download Video. Real-time progress, speed (MB/s), and ETA are displayed.
  6. Once complete, switch to the Library tab to preview the file or click Open in AI Clip Studio to start clipping immediately.
  • Navigate to YouTube Desk (/youtube-desk).
  • In the Channel Overview header, click Connect Channel to authorize UpClip Studio using your Google account and OAuth credentials.
  • In the Upload / Schedule tab, select the video file you exported from AI Clip Studio or Caption Studio.
  • Fill in title, description, tags, category, and privacy status (Public, Unlisted, or Private).
  • Choose Publish Immediately or pick a future date and time under Schedule for Later.
  • Monitor your active uploads in the Upload Queue. Filter tasks by status (All, Uploading, Processing, Scheduled, Failed) and retry any interrupted jobs with a single click.
